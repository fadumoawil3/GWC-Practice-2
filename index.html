About GWC

Girls Who Code is a nonprofit organization on a mission to close the gender gap in technology. We believe every girl deserves the opportunity to learn how to build technology that can shape her future—and the world. Our community provides a place where girls feel safe, inspired, and empowered to explore coding and leadership.

Founded in 2012, the organization has impacted hundreds of thousands of girls globally. We work with schools, libraries, and companies to reach as many students as possible with our mission.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to inspire, educate, and equip girls with the computing skills to pursue 21st-century opportunities. We envision a future where more women are not only using technology, but creating it—changing how our world works for the better.

By fostering curiosity, creativity, and resilience, we aim to redefine what a coder looks like and break down stereotypes in tech spaces. Whether in clubs or online, girls learn they belong in tech.

Programs Offered

  • Clubs: Free after-school programs for 3rd–12th grade girls to explore computer science in a supportive environment.
  • Summer Immersion Program: Two-week coding intensives at major tech companies and universities, providing hands-on experience and mentorship.
  • College Loops: Campus chapters where college students build sisterhood and pursue careers in computer science together.

Each program focuses on sisterhood, real-world coding skills, and confidence-building, creating lasting impact in the lives of young women everywhere.
